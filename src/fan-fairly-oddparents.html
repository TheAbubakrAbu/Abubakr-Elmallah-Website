The Fairly OddParents

Timmy Turner is ten, nobody listens to him, and that misery is the exact qualification that gets him two fairy godparents. He can wish for anything. The wish is always granted precisely as worded, and that is the whole joke: the magic never malfunctions, it just does what he actually said instead of what he meant.

Holding it together is a thick rulebook with a loophole on every page, a teacher who is completely correct about the fairies and will never be believed, and Vicky, a sixteen-year-old babysitter who is sweet to the parents and runs the house like a prison the second they leave. Ten seasons of a kid getting exactly what he asked for.
