CliftonStrengths Maximizer · Relator · Positivity

The Student Center & Event Services team took Gallup’s CliftonStrengths assessment, which ranks 34 themes and gives you your top five. Mine came out Maximizer, Relator, Positivity, Communication and Adaptability: four of the five sit in Gallup’s yellow domain, influencing and relationship-building rather than analysis, which surprised me for someone who writes code all day.

Everyone made a name tent for their desk. One felt like a waste of a good excuse, so I made three, each a LEGO character who fit the yellow band: Anakin Skywalker, the Gold Ninja from Ninjago, and Mr. Gold, the rarest minifigure LEGO ever made.
