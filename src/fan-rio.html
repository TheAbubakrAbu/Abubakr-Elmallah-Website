Rio

A macaw from Minnesota who cannot fly, flown to Brazil to meet the only other one of his kind left alive. Carlos Saldanha made it about the city he grew up in, and the whole film is an excuse to animate Carnival.

The bird is real, and its story is worse than the film’s. The last known wild Spix’s macaw went in 2000, and BirdLife International formally declared the species extinct in the wild in 2019; every one left was in captivity. Rio came out in 2011 and helped fund the breeding programme, and in 2022 they started putting them back into the caatinga. The San Diego Zoo page on this site is about the same job done at scale.
