Rio

A Spix's macaw raised in Minnesota is flown to Rio de Janeiro to save his species, discovers he cannot fly, and spends the film chained to the one other one left. Carlos Saldanha directed it and he is from Rio, which is most of why the city in it is a place rather than a postcard.

The uncomfortable part is the fact underneath. The Spix's macaw was declared extinct in the wild in 2019, three years after the second film, and the species survives only because of captive birds. The premise of a children's cartoon turned out to be a documentary.
