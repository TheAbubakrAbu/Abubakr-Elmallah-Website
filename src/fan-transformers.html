Franchise · As a kid · since 1984

Transformers

A toy that is honestly two toys: a car that folds into a robot out of exactly the same parts, with nothing added and nothing left over. Everything else, four decades of cartoons and seven live-action films, exists to sell that one trick, and the trick is good enough to have carried all of it.

I came in through the Bay films and through Transformers: The Ride at Universal, which is the thing that actually got me the first time I went: a motion base on a track through 3D screens, on the Lower Lot, before I knew a single character's name. The 1984 material I found backwards, years later, which is the wrong order and probably the usual one.
