Nintendo

A playing-card company founded in Kyoto in 1889 that then spent eighty years failing at taxis, instant rice and a love hotel before it worked out what it was for. Everything since runs on one rule: find the toy first, and build the game around it.

It is also the only one of the three console makers that keeps refusing to compete on the axis the other two picked. No arms race on resolution, a controller nobody asked for every generation, and a first-party catalogue deep enough that it has never had to.

Mario, Zelda and Splatoon are all on this page rather than three of their own. They are one company, one machine at a time and the same afternoons, and splitting them meant introducing the same launch-week Switch three times over.
