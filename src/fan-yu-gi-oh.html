Franchise · As a kid · since 1996

Yu-Gi-Oh!

Kazuki Takahashi wrote a manga about a boy who plays games, and one of the games in it was a card game that appeared in a single chapter. It got the loudest response of anything in the series, so it took over the whole thing, and then it left the page: the real Yu-Gi-Oh! trading card game has sold over twenty-five billion cards.

The anime is a strange document. It is a show about children playing a card game, animated as though every turn is a duel to the death, with a narrator who explains the rules and a protagonist who is possessed by an Egyptian pharaoh. It should not work at any level and it ran for twenty-five years.
