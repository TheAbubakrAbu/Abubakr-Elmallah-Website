The studs

Every LEGO game runs on the same four studs, and the values never changed across twenty years and thirty-five titles. Smash anything at all and they come out of it, which is the actual core loop, and the reason you end up demolishing a whole hub area for no reason.

Why it clutches

The studs on top press against the tubes underneath and the walls between them. That single idea, patented in 1958, is the reason a brick from that year still grips one moulded this morning.

Every dimension in the system is a multiple of the same numbers, which is why a wheel, a hinge, a minifigure hand and a spaceship canopy all agree with each other sixty-seven years later.
