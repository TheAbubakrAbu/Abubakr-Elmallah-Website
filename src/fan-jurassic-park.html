Welcome… to Jurassic Park. John Hammond · 1993

A mosquito in amber, a hundred million years old, with a belly full of dinosaur blood. Everything in six films comes out of one drop of it, and out of the gaps in that genome, which they fill with frog DNA because it was the nearest thing to hand. Nobody in the room stops to ask what else frogs can do.

Fourteen minutes of dinosaur across two hours, most of it a nine-thousand-pound hydraulic machine being rained on, and it has never once been bettered. Life, uh, finds a way.
