The Security Office

The whole site watched from one desk. Every page is a camera feed, the monitor pulls up one at a time, and checking the cameras drains the power exactly the way it does in the game. Run it down to nothing and the lights go out, which here just means the page tells you so.

A horror game made of security cameras and a battery meter, which is ruthlessly efficient design: the whole thing is a resource you are spending to feel safe. Nothing on this page is a screenshot or a character; every feed is drawn.
