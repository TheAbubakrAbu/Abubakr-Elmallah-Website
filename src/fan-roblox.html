Roblox

Not a game. An engine, a marketplace and a chat client, where everything you played was made by somebody else who was not being paid a salary, and quite often was about your age. That is why it looks like nothing in particular: there is no art direction, because there is no art director.

The thing worth saying about it is the tool. Roblox Studio ships free, it is the same editor the platform is built on, and it scripts in Luau, a real typed language that got open-sourced in 2021. For an enormous number of people my age, the first programming language they ever touched was that one, and they touched it because they wanted a better obby.
