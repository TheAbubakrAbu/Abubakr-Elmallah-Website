Franchise · Secondary · since 2011

Ninjago

Somehow a LEGO toy line grew into a genuinely good sixteen-season saga. Six ninja, one element each, and a continuity most franchises would envy.

All worlds ↗ Everything in it ↗
Ninja-GO! Kai · Master of Fire

The Sword of Fire, forged by the First Spinjitzu Master out of the same power he used to build the world, and hidden in the Fire Temple with a dragon sitting on it. It melts through stone, and Kai, a blacksmith before he was ever a ninja, is the only one it was ever going to go to.

A LEGO toy line that had no business becoming a sixteen-season saga with real continuity, real stakes and characters who visibly grow up. It has been running since 2011 and it never got worse.

Spinjitzu

Spinjitzu

Unlock your true potential, spin, and the element comes with you: fire red, lightning blue, earth black, ice white, water silver, and green for the one who can use all of them.

The show sold it as a toy gimmick first, a spinner you actually bought in a shop, then spent sixteen seasons building a mythology underneath it: the First Spinjitzu Master, the four golden weapons, the realms, and a prophecy the whole thing pays off.

The Four Golden Weaponsforged by the First Spinjitzu Master

Made out of the same power he used to create Ninjago, split up because no one person should hold all four, and hidden across the map behind a dragon each. Season one is one long fetch quest for these, and it is a very good one.
