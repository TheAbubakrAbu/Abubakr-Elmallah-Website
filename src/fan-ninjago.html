Spinjitzu

Spinjitzu

Unlock your true potential, spin, and the element comes with you: fire red, lightning blue, earth black, ice white, water silver, and green for the one who can use all of them.

The show sold it as a toy gimmick first, a spinner you actually bought in a shop, then spent sixteen seasons building a mythology underneath it: the First Spinjitzu Master, the four golden weapons, the realms, and a prophecy the whole thing pays off.
