The Hunger Games

A children’s series about a televised child-killing contest, which is a sentence the trilogy spends three books earning. Katniss wins the first book by refusing to finish it, on camera, and everything after that is about who was watching.

Panem is named after panem et circenses: bread and circuses. Collins named the country after the mechanism, and the series never pretends the fighting is the subject.
