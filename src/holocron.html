The eras on file

Everything canon sits in one of these. The Skywalker story is about fifty years wide; the galaxy around it runs for twenty-five thousand.

  1. 25,000 BBYThe Republic is foundedThe Jedi Order and the Republic grow up together, and neither works out how to be separate.
  2. 382 – 82 BBYThe High RepublicThe Order at its height, expanding into the frontier, with the Nihil waiting in the Drift.
  3. 32 – 19 BBYThe Fall of the JediA Republic votes itself into an Empire and applauds while it happens.
  4. 19 – 5 BBYReign of the EmpireInquisitors hunt the survivors. Rebel cells form on a hundred worlds that never meet.
  5. 5 BBY – 4 ABYAge of RebellionScarif, then Yavin: two Death Stars and an Emperor, all started by stolen plans.
  6. 4 – 28 ABYThe New RepublicA government that will not admit the war is not over, and a Mandalorian in the middle of it.
  7. 28 – 35 ABYRise of the First OrderStarkiller Base, Crait, Exegol, and the last Skywalker deciding to be one on purpose.
