Angry Birds

A projectile-motion problem with a grudge attached. One control, no tutorial, and a physics engine doing all the writing: you pull back, you let go, and the interesting part is which way the building falls.

Rovio’s fifty-second game, made by a company that was nearly out of money, sold for a dollar in December 2009. The three-star rating turned a five-minute game into a hundred-hour one without adding a single mechanic.
