Every room on this site, as a store in Starcourt Mall or a cabinet in the Palace Arcade. Point at one, tab to it, or on a phone scroll it into the middle of the screen, and the bulbs on Joyce Byers’ wall light one at a time to spell it out, the way Will talked through them from the other side.

Start at the mall. Keep scrolling and the town goes over: the lights go cold, the vines come in, and by the bottom of the page you are reading the same rooms from the other side. The switch on the wall takes you there at once if you would rather not walk.
