The only major superhero with no powers at all. Everything he has, he bought or built or trained for, which is exactly why he is the one that stuck: the fantasy is not that you were born special, it is that you decided to be and then did the work.

Eighty-odd years, and the character survives every reinterpretation (camp, gothic, grounded, detective, brawler) because the core is stable and the tone is the variable.
