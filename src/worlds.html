Franchises I Love, no apps, just taste

70 tiles, ranked the way I actually rank them. Who I am comes first, and none of it is a fandom: my faith, my language and where my family is from are not things I chose off a shelf, and everything under them makes more sense once they are on the page. Then the three I know by heart, the ones I have lived in, regular rotation, the rest, the ones that were everything to me as a kid and are not what I put on now, and the theme parks at the end. Every one of them has its own page now, click a tile. Apart from Islam and Star Wars at the top, none of these ship an app; every tile is set in type and drawn by hand, with no studio logos, no posters and no photographs.
