Teen Titans Go!

Five superheroes who have stopped doing superheroics and now argue about waffles. It is eleven minutes long, it resets every episode, and it has run for nine seasons, which is roughly seven times as long as the serious version it came from.

The part everybody skips when they are being angry about it: the five leads are played by the same five actors who played them in the 2003 Teen Titans. Same voices, same characters, pointed at jokes instead of a Slade arc. That is why the crossover film works, and why the theatrical one in 2018 turned out to be genuinely good.
