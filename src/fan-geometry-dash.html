Game · As a kid · since 2013

Geometry Dash

One button, no health bar, and no checkpoints inside a level. Touch anything and the attempt ends from the first frame, which means the only thing the game can teach you is the level itself, one death at a time.

It was written by one person in Sweden, and the part that outgrew him is the editor. Twenty-two official levels shipped; the community has built millions, some of them so hard that a single verified completion is a piece of news.
