Skylanders

Put the figure on the portal and it appears in the game, with its own level, its own health bar and whatever you left it on last time stored in a chip in its base. Toys for Bob invented the whole toys-to-life category with this in 2011, and Disney Infinity, amiibo and LEGO Dimensions all followed it.

It also had Hans Zimmer and Lorne Balfe writing the main theme, for a game about collectible plastic dragons, which tells you exactly how much money Activision thought was in this. They were right for about four years.
