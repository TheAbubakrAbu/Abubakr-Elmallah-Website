Looney Tunes

Warner Bros' answer to Disney, and funnier than it: seven minutes, no moral, no songs worth speaking of, and a cast who mostly win by staying calm while somebody else destroys themselves. Nearly every voice in the building was one man.

The directors are why it lasted. Chuck Jones built rules and then kept them, the Road Runner never leaving the road and Wile E. never speaking; Tex Avery broke every rule there was, including the edge of the frame; Bob Clampett drew like the paper was on fire. Same studio, three completely different comedies.
