Vought International, marketing

The premise is one swap: superheroes are not myth, they are inventory. A pharmaceutical company manufactures them, owns the film rights, runs the news channel that covers them, and keeps a crisis team on retainer for when one of them kills somebody. Nobody was born with anything: every hero on the shelf was made in a lab and sold a story about destiny afterwards.

That is a much scarier idea than a villain, and it is why the show works long after the shock wears off. The horror is never the powers. It is the PR department that exists to make the powers palatable, and the fact that it is very, very good at its job.
