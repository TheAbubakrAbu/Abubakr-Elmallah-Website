Star Wars

No apps on this page. This is just the galaxy: where the eras sit on the timeline, why the prequels and the Clone Wars are the ones I keep going back to, what the kyber colours mean, and the lines I have not stopped quoting since I was ten.

It is a period of civil war. Rebel spaceships, striking from a hidden base, have won their first victory against the evil Galactic Empire. And a kid in Mission Viejo has watched it enough times to know the crawl by heart.

An elegant weapon, for a more civilized age. Obi-Wan Kenobi · A New Hope

A kyber crystal, a focusing array and a power cell, in a hilt every Jedi builds by hand as the last step of their training. No two are alike, because the crystal picks its Jedi and the Jedi builds the rest of it around what the crystal wants.

It is a terrible weapon in almost every practical sense: no range, no ammunition, and it will take your own arm off. It exists because the people carrying it were supposed to be the kind who would rather not need it at all.

The Atlasevery world, drawn in CSS

Every world from the films, the shows and the games, sorted by era, the way my Datapad app sorts them. Each planet is drawn entirely in CSS: no images, just gradients, a rotating surface layer, and its own atmosphere. Pick an era, and hover one to scan it.
