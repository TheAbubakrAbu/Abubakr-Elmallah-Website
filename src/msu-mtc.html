MSU & MTC

The two organizations I have given the most time to at UC Irvine. In the Muslim Student Union I ran the brothers’ social calendar for a year and then moved to dawah; in the Muslim Tech Collaborative I co-founded the UCI chapter and worked up from VP of Workshops to Vice President. The roles themselves sit on College; this page is the record of what actually ran.

Dawah Coordinator MSU · 2026–27

Third year, and the MSU position moved from the social side to dawah. Two things run under it: a weekly booth on Ring Road where anyone can ask about Islam, and the Domino Process workshop, which builds the case for Islam one step at a time, each premise knocking over the next.
