Franchise · As a kid · since 2002

Ice Age

Blue Sky’s first film, and the one that made the studio: three animals who cannot stand each other carrying a human baby across a continent. It works because the herd is genuinely mismatched and the film never pretends otherwise.

And then there is Scrat, who is in a completely different film. He has no dialogue, no connection to the plot and no chance of ever getting the acorn, and he opens and closes every one of these. He is the best silent-comedy character animation has produced since Chuck Jones, and he is a bolt-on.
