Bloons TD

Monkeys throwing darts at balloons. That is the whole premise, and Ninja Kiwi have spent nearly twenty years turning it into a genuinely deep game: every tower has three upgrade paths, you can only take two of them far, and the balloons keep layering into harder balloons underneath the ones you just popped.

Bloons TD 6 is the one that stuck. It shipped in 2018 and is still getting updates, which for a Flash-game sequel is faintly ridiculous, and the community has taken it somewhere the original browser version never suggested it could go.
