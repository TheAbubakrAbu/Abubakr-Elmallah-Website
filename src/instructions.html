Every page of the site, one step at a time

The whole site rebuilt as the booklet that comes in the box: every page is a numbered step, every step lists the parts it takes, and you build it in order or skip to whichever bag you feel like opening. Nothing here is a photograph: every brick on this page is drawn with CSS and SVG, the same way the rest of these interfaces are.

I grew up on the LEGO games far more than the sets, which is why there is a whole catalog of them on the LEGO world page. This is the other half of it.
