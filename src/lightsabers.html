Lightsaber Guide

Mostly for me. Every saber I own runs on one button, and every board counts the holds differently. Proffie, SNV4 and SD RGB are Theory Sabers; the Xenopixel is Imperial Workshop. The Theory boards are from Theory’s own support page.
