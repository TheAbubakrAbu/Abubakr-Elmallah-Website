(Showcase)Faith, built with care

Al-Islam

A family of Islamic apps for the whole Apple ecosystem: an all-in-one companion, a beginner-friendly Quran, and precise prayer times. Two of them earned national recognition.

{% include glance.html %}

The Engineswhat the apps are built on

The data layers underneath. Both exist because the alternative was hand-marking a text that runs to six thousand verses, or scraping someone else’s HTML.

Not on the App Storeone still coming, one retired

OC Ummah is the newest of these and is not out yet. ICOI is the oldest: the first app I ever shipped, built for my own mosque in my senior year of high school and maintained for two and a half years, until the mosque moved to another app. It is also what led to teaching iOS at the Muslim Village.

Livethe apps, running in this page

Rather than describe what Al-Adhan and Al-Islam do, here they are doing it. The prayer times below are computed in your browser from the sun’s position for the coordinates shown; there is no API behind this and nothing about your location leaves the page. The qiblah is the true great-circle bearing to the Kaʿbah from the same point, which is why it does not simply point east.

Tajwīdالتجويد

The rules that govern how the Qurʾān is pronounced, shown the way Al-Quran shows them: as colour on the letters themselves. My Quran Tajweed Engine derives this for all 6,236 verses from the rules rather than from a marked-up text; below is Sūrat al-Ikhlāṣ, four verses of it, marked by hand so each colour can be explained. Pick a single rule to find just that one in the line.
