(Showcase)Faith, built with care

Al-Islam

A family of Islamic apps for the whole Apple ecosystem: an all-in-one companion, a beginner-friendly Quran, and precise prayer times. Two of them earned national recognition.

The Engineswhat the apps are built on

The data layers underneath. Both exist because the alternative was hand-marking a text that runs to six thousand verses, or scraping someone else’s HTML.

وَيَمۡكُرُونَ وَيَمۡكُرُ ٱللَّهُ وَٱللَّهُ خَيۡرُ ٱلۡمَٰكِرِينَ

“But they plan, and Allah plans. And Allah is the best of planners.”

Holy Quran · Al-Anfal 8:30

The Three Holy Mosquesالحرمان والأقصى

The three mosques the Prophet ﷺ said are worth setting out to travel for: Makkah, Madinah, and Jerusalem. Every mosque here is drawn, not photographed: each one is an SVG elevation built from the same parts, in its own architectural language.

Prophets in the Quranالأنبياء

Twenty-five are named. Grouped the way the Quran tends to introduce them — the first prophets, the ones sent to the Arab peoples, the house of Ibrāhīm, the Israelites who came through Isḥāq, and finally the one who came through Ismāʿīl. Filter by line.

وَمَا أَرْسَلْنَا مِن رَسُولٍ إِلَّا بِلِسَانِ قَوْمِهِ لِيُبَيِّنَ لَهُم

“And We did not send any messenger except in the language of his people, so that he could make things clear to them.”

Holy Quran · Ibrāhīm 14:4

Every one of them was sent speaking the language of the people in front of him. Five are the Ulul-ʿAzm, those of firm resolve — Nūḥ, Ibrāhīm, Mūsā, ʿĪsā and Muḥammad ﷺ — and they are tagged below.

Scholarsthe athari chain

The line the ummah’s knowledge actually travelled down: the rightly guided caliphs, the ten promised Jannah, the Muhājirūn and the Anṣār, the two generations after them, then the four imāms, the ḥadīth masters, the writers of ʿaqīdah and tafsīr, Ibn Taymiyyah’s circle in Damascus, and the revivalists after them. Filter by generation.

خَيْرُ النَّاسِ قَرْنِي، ثُمَّ الَّذِينَ يَلُونَهُمْ، ثُمَّ الَّذِينَ يَلُونَهُمْ

“The best of people are my generation, then those who come after them, then those who come after them.”

Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī · Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim

Those three generations are as-Salaf aṣ-Ṣāliḥ: the Companions, the Tābiʿūn who met them, and the Atbāʿ at-Tābiʿīn after them. Every group tagged Salaf below is one of the three; everyone listed after them is measured against what they understood.
