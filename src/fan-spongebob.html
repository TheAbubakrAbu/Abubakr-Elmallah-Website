SpongeBob

A sea sponge with a spatula, drawn by an actual marine biologist, in a show whose first three seasons are some of the tightest comedy writing ever animated. The jokes were built to work twice (once when you are seven, again when you are twenty) and they do.

It also became something nobody planned: the shared second language of the internet. Half the reaction images in any group chat are screenshots of this show, and everyone understands all of them without ever being taught. That is cultural infrastructure.
