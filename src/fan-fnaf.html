Phone Guy, night one

A horror game made of security cameras and a battery meter, built by one person in a few months, and it reset what a horror game needed to be. You cannot move, you cannot fight, and you cannot even look at two places at once. Every single thing you do costs power, and the power is the clock.

That constraint is the whole design. Checking a camera costs you. Closing a door costs you more. So the game is really a resource-management puzzle wearing a jump-scare costume, and the reason it works is that you always know exactly how you died, and it was always something you chose.
