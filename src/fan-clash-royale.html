Clash Royale

Three minutes, two lanes, three towers, and an elixir bar that refills whether you are ready or not. Clash Royale is chess where the clock never stops and the pieces walk on their own: the cleanest competitive design ever shipped to a phone.

And it did not come from nowhere: every barbarian, giant and goblin in the arena marched out of Clash of Clans first. The village game lives further down this page, because the two are one universe and one childhood.
