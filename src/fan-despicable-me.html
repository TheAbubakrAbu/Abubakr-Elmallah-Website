Despicable Me

A supervillain adopts three orphans so he can walk up to a rival’s front door, and then cannot give them back. That is the whole first film, and it is a much smaller and better story than anything the franchise has done since.

What happened next is the interesting bit. The henchmen in the background were yellow, indestructible and spoke no real language, so they needed no translation anywhere on earth. Seven films later this is the highest-grossing animated franchise ever made, and Universal built the front of two parks around it.
