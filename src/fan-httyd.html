How to Train Your Dragon

A scrawny viking shoots down the one dragon nobody has ever seen, finds it alive with half its tail missing, and instead of killing it, builds it a new fin. Everything great about the trilogy is already in that decision: it is a story where the hero’s superpower is noticing, and then engineering.

It also has the best flight scenes ever animated and John Powell’s best score, which is not a coincidence, because they are the same thing.
