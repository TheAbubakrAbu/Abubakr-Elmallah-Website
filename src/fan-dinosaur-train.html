Dinosaur Train

A Tyrannosaurus egg ends up in a Pteranodon nest, hatches, and the family simply gets on with it. That is an adoption story told to four-year-olds without a single heavy sentence in it, and the dinosaurs are the sugar around the outside.

The train runs through tunnels that come out in the Triassic, the Jurassic and the Cretaceous, which is the cleverest thing in the show: two animals that never overlapped can meet without the program lying about when either of them lived. Craig Bartlett, who made Hey Arnold!, created it; the Jim Henson Company produced it; and a real paleontologist turns up on camera at the end of segments to say which parts were true.
