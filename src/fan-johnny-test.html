Johnny Test

Johnny is eleven, wants a shortcut to everything, and happens to live above a laboratory run by his thirteen-year-old twin sisters, who are both genuine geniuses. The trade is the entire show: they will build him whatever he asks for, provided he agrees to be the one they test it on. He always agrees.

His dog Dukey can talk, courtesy of an earlier experiment, and spends every episode as the only character who can see what is about to happen. What people actually remember is the sound design: a whip-crack on every scene transition, for six seasons, loud enough that you could hear which show was on from another room.
