Franchise · As a kid · since 1999

Beyblade

Takara made a spinning top with interchangeable parts and a rip cord, then made an anime about children who take it extremely seriously, and the two sold each other for twenty-five years. Let it rip.

The reason it worked longer than any other toy-anime pairing is that the toy is actually a system: attack, defense, stamina and balance types, weight rings, spin tracks, performance tips, and a real trade-off every time you build one. It is a customisation game about angular momentum, sold to eight-year-olds.
