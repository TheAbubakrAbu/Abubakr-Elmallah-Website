Game · Tertiary · since 2015

Splatoon

A shooter you win by covering the floor rather than by killing anybody, which is the single design decision that let Nintendo make one at all. Four on four, three minutes, and the score at the end is the percentage of the ground that is your colour.

It also solved a problem nobody else had. Your own ink is a liquid you swim through, faster and hidden and reloading while you do it, so painting is the objective and the movement system at the same time. Every weapon, map and special in the game falls out of that one idea.
