DuckTales

Donald has to go away, so three nephews are parked with a great-uncle nobody in the family speaks to any more, who happens to be the richest duck in the world and keeps a building full of money on a hill above the town. Everything for the next hundred episodes is that duck failing to hold three children at arm’s length.

It started as comics: Carl Barks invented Scrooge McDuck in 1947 for a single Christmas story, was not allowed to sign his name, and ended up inventing Duckburg, the Money Bin, Gyro, Magica and the Beagle Boys before anyone knew who he was. The 1987 series was Disney’s first big-budget show made for daily syndication, the 2017 reboot went back past it to the comics and was better than a reboot needed to be, and a Capcom tie-in on the NES wrote a piece of music that has outlasted the lot.
