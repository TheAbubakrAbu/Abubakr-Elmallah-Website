(Worlds I have played)Four Steam Replays2022–2025

Gaming

The third page here that is about me rather than about somebody else's world: Travels is where I have been, Accents is what I sound like, and this is what I have played. It is built out of receipts rather than opinions, because for once there are receipts: Steam writes every account a Replay card each December, and I captured four of mine before they went away.

Every game itself lives on the page of the world it belongs to, and none of those lists are copied here: what the top of this page carries is the part that belongs to no single world, which is four years counted, every completion shelf I have, and the index to the seventeen worlds with games in them.

Underneath that is every screenshot I have taken in a game, all of it, one section per franchise: Minecraft, Star Wars, LEGO, the Wizarding World and the rest. That part writes itself. The page lists whatever is in the folder at build time, so a new picture only has to be dropped in and encoded to turn up here in its own section, with a caption of its own if it has been written one.
