Thirty-five years and eight hundred episodes, and it is still the show every other animated comedy is measured against. The golden age (roughly seasons three to nine) wrote a genuinely enormous share of the jokes the internet still runs on, and did it while being sincerely about a family that loves each other.

Pink frosting, rainbow sprinkles, and a man who would trade his soul for one. He did, actually, in Treehouse of Horror IV, and Marge got it back because she had it in a locket.
