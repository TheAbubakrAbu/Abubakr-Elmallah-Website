Phineas and Ferb

A hundred and four days of summer vacation, and two stepbrothers who build something impossible before lunch every single day. A roller coaster, a beach in the back garden, a one-hit wonder, a haunted house. Their sister tries to tell on them and the evidence is always gone by the time she gets back with an adult.

Underneath the formula is the joke that makes it work: their pet platypus is a secret agent, and the reason the evidence always disappears is that he has just blown up whatever their mother’s neighbour built that morning. The two plots never know about each other and they resolve each other every time, for a hundred and twenty-nine episodes.
