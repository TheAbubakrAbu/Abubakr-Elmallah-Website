The Maze Runner

A boy comes up in a lift with no memory except his name. There are fifty other boys up there, a field, a forest, and four stone doors into a maze that changes itself every night and has never been solved in three years of trying.

The trick of the first book is that it withholds nothing unfairly: Thomas knows as little as you do, and everything he works out, he works out on the page. The sequels swap the maze for a desert and then a city, and the series never quite gets that first idea back.
