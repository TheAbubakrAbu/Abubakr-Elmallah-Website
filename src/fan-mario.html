Franchise · Tertiary · since 1981

Mario

Everything starts with the jump. Miyamoto built the movement before the character and before the story: a run that has weight, an arc you can steer in mid-air, and a landing you can feel. Mario existed as a set of rules months before he had a name, which is why he still feels right in a game made forty years later.

He is the most-appearing character in games and the only one who works in every genre anyone has thrown him into: platformers, karts, tennis, a paper-based role-playing game, a turn-based strategy crossover with the Rabbids. The Nintendo page on this site is the company; this one is the plumber.
