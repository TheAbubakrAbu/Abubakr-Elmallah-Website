Accents

This is all of them in the order I picked them up, which turns out to be a fairly accurate map of what I was watching each year of school. Each card has the phrase I actually use to get into it and the one sound that carries the whole thing.

The recordings are not up yet. Every card is already wired for one, so they will appear here as I get them made rather than as a separate page.
