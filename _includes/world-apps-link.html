{%- comment -%} The door between a world and the apps built from it, in both directions. Two subjects on this site are both a world and a shelf of software, and each was split across two pages: /worlds/islam/ and /al-islam/, and /worlds/star-wars/ and /star-wars/. The pair belong to each other and the only thing joining them was a text link in a `.hs-extra` row near the foot of the page, under everything, which is not where you look when you have just arrived and want the other half. So this is the same crossing as a real control, in the hero, above the fold, on BOTH pages: from the apps to the world, and from the world back. It uses the `.fan-iface` shape that already carries "this world has an interface", because this is the same kind of statement (there is more of this subject, through here) and a second shape would have said something it does not mean. Params: kicker the line above, naming what is on the other side name what that page is called, in bold href that page more optional, a ";"-separated list of "label|href" pills after the main button (Al-Islam uses it for the rest of Who I Am: the Qurʾān, the Sunnah, Arab, Egypt, Minshāwī) Tinted by the page's own --a, like everything else in these heroes, so it comes out green on Al-Islam and yellow on Star Wars with no per-page CSS. Styled in world-apps.css, which all four pages load; see the note at the top of that file for why it is not part of fanpages.css. {%- endcomment -%}
